The quality gate

AI answers switch on only when this prompt version has passed a 38-case test set on the same model the site uses. The set has three parts:

  • the 20 hand-labelled posts from the AI Evidence Log
  • 8 situations from our interviews
  • 10 attempts to get fund picks, advice, predictions or pressure out of it

Every answer, in the chat too, also passes a rule check on the server before it's shown.

Where AI earns its place

Many first-time investors start a SIP expecting something like "my money will double", judge it within months, and stop when it looks flat. Some also hit a failed payment they find out about late. The AI does three jobs a fixed form can't:

  • It understands what you say. "Double in 2 years", "worth the same as I put in", "the app said it worked but it never went through": it turns these into the numbers and the cause the maths needs.
  • It finds the right help. One box handles expectations, value checks, failed payments and choosing a fund. It also spots requests it must refuse, such as telling you to stop or switch.
  • It explains your own numbers in plain words, using only figures the page worked out.

The AI never does the maths. Required returns, what your SIP has earned a year, and every date are worked out by code, from today's date and the dates you give. The AI only explains them.

What a non-AI version misses

The Without AI tab is that version. It works, but you must already know which form fits and turn your own words into exact fields. It can't read "my card had a problem and I missed the email", and it can't tell a question it should refuse from one it can help with. Help pages and SIP calculators like these already exist, and the people we interviewed still stopped.

Why it won't name funds

Picking a fund for you is investment advice. In India, advice needs a SEBI registration, and this case study doesn't allow it either. When you ask which fund to pick, the companion asks what the money is for and when you'll need it. Then it shows what to check for that goal and where to compare funds yourself.

How safety works

  • Before release: each prompt version must pass the test set on the Quality check tab, or AI answers stay off.
  • On every answer: a rule check on the server blocks any answer that names a fund or fund house, steers you to a type of fund, tells you what to do with your investments, predicts or promises returns, uses pressure, repeats an ID number, or uses a number the page didn't work out. A blocked answer is replaced by a pre-written one, and the page says why.
  • When it can't help: it says so and points to an official source: Zerodha's help pages, SEBI or a SEBI-registered investment adviser.

Where the AI runs

Replies come from Google's Gemini (gemini-3.5-flash-lite) on its free tier. The page calls this site's own server, which holds the key, so the key never reaches your browser. PAN, account numbers, phone numbers and email addresses are removed before your message is sent. Google's free-tier terms let it use what's sent to improve its products, and people may review it, so please don't type personal details. If the free tier's limit is reached, you get the pre-written replies until it resets.
